CodeClear is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government or emergency service. It does not issue official alerts. Follow the original system warning and the issuing authority's instructions.

Where the displayed text comes from

After separate permission, CodeClear reads individual alert text exposed by reviewed emergency-alert system applications installed on your Android device, through notification access or visible alert dialogs through accessibility access. It does not fetch an official alert feed from the websites below. Availability depends on the installed receiver and firmware. A system application identifier alone does not authenticate the government agency that issued an individual message.

Nearby Bluetooth messages come from other users and have unverified origin and authenticity. Translations are generated by the engine you select and may be inaccurate. Neither a translation nor a nearby message is an official government statement. CodeClear cannot guarantee complete or timely alerts.

Official information and verification

Use the authority named in the original message and its official channels to verify information. These examples provide public emergency information; they do not establish CodeClear support for every device or country, or verify a particular peer message.

  • South Korea: Ministry of the Interior and Safety public safety portal — emergency messages and safety guidance.
  • United States: FEMA Wireless Emergency Alerts — information about official wireless alerts.
  • Japan: Japan Meteorological Agency — Earthquake Early Warning information and safety guidance.
  • Germany: Federal Office of Civil Protection and Disaster Assistance — Cell Broadcast warning information.
  • France: FR-Alert — the government's public warning service and published alerts.
  • Taiwan: National Science and Technology Center for Disaster Reduction — public disaster information and official resources.
  • China: Ministry of Emergency Management — public emergency information and safety guidance.
