Data deletion

English한국어

Effective November 3, 2026.

You can delete local history and the authenticated CodeClear server cache separately. Removing the app or its access token does not delete existing server data.

Delete on your device

Delete a record from History, or open Settings → Privacy and delete all local records. Downloaded ML Kit models have separate removal actions in model management. Android app storage removal or uninstall removes CodeClear's private local data.

Delete the CodeClear service cache

  1. Keep your saved access token until cache deletion is complete.
  2. Open Settings → Labs → CodeClear server and choose the server cache deletion action.
  3. Confirm the request and wait for its result. This deletes the cache scope authenticated by that token, including stored originals and translated results.
  4. You may then turn the service off or remove the token. Your device history is separate.

Request help or deletion by email

Email gokirito12@gmail.com with the subject “CodeClear data deletion”. State whether you need help with the CodeClear service cache or support correspondence. Do not send access tokens, provider keys or alert bodies. We may need to verify the relevant service access before acting on a request.

What may be retained

Active translation results expire within 30 days of creation. Cache deletion does not promise immediate erasure of operational logs, backups, security counters or deletion-generation records. These have separate operational or legal retention; contact the privacy email for the applicable information and deletion assistance. Support messages are retained while needed for the request, necessary follow-up or legal obligations.

CodeClear cannot erase data already processed by Groq or MyMemory, or copies received through sharing. Their separate practices and the SDK diagnostics disclosures are explained in the privacy policy. The app has no in-app account creation.
